How Does Getting Paid By YouTube Work? Show AnswerHide Answer

YouTube runs ads on your videos and pays you monthly. You need 1,000 subscribers and 10M Shorts views to switch it on.

  • 10 million views sounds mad, but one Short can do it alone. Our student Lucas posted a single video that did 21.6 million
  • Once you're in, videos you posted months ago keep paying while you're at work or asleep
  • YouTube pays a different rate per view depending on what your videos are about, and some topics pay several times more than others. That's exactly why we pick yours for you
  • People lose payments for breaking rules they never read. We set yours up so it doesn't happen
